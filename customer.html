Anytime Fitness – Membership Information

A quick guide to your membership, facilities and services

Member Guide

Welcome to Anytime Fitness! Your membership gives you access to a convenient fitness environment designed to support your health and wellness goals. Whether you want to improve your strength, build endurance, maintain your fitness, or develop a consistent workout routine, Anytime Fitness provides the facilities, equipment, and support to help you along the way.

1 Membership Benefits
  • 24/7 Gym Access: Enjoy round-the-clock access to your home gym, allowing you to work out at a time that fits your schedule.
  • Personalized Fitness Support: Receive guidance from professional coaches who can help you develop workout plans based on your fitness goals.
  • Workout Resources: Explore different workout routines, exercise guides, and fitness resources.
  • Progress Tracking: Monitor your fitness progress and stay informed about your workouts and personal goals.
2 Gym Facilities and Equipment

Members can enjoy access to a variety of gym equipment and facilities, including:

  • Strength Equipment: Squat racks, barbells, dumbbells, adjustable benches, and strength machines.
  • Cardio Equipment: Treadmills, stationary bikes, ellipticals, rowing machines, and stair climbers.
  • Functional Training Equipment: Kettlebells, resistance bands, medicine balls, battle ropes, and other training equipment.
  • Member Facilities: Private restrooms, showers, storage cubbies, Wi-Fi, and water fountains.

Members can access participating Anytime Fitness locations worldwide using their assigned key fob.

Your home gym serves as your primary membership location, where you can manage your membership and request assistance.

4 Coaching and Personal Training Services

Anytime Fitness offers various coaching services to help members work toward their fitness goals, including:

  • Fitness Consultations: Discuss your fitness goals and receive guidance on getting started.
  • Personalized Workout Plans: Follow exercise routines designed around your individual goals.
  • Personal Training: Receive one-on-one guidance from a professional fitness coach.
  • Nutrition and Recovery Guidance: Learn about nutrition, recovery, and mobility as part of your overall fitness journey.
  • Equipment Orientation: Become familiar with gym equipment and how to use it appropriately.
5 Important Membership Reminders
  • Keep your membership information and account details updated.
  • Use your assigned key fob when entering the gym.
  • Follow gym rules, equipment guidelines, and safety procedures.
  • Return equipment to its designated area after use.
  • Respect other members and maintain cleanliness throughout the facility.
  • Contact your home gym through support tickets for questions regarding membership renewals, payments, contract terms, and account concerns.
  • Check with gym staff for specific membership policies, available services, and applicable fees.

Make Every Workout Count!

Anytime Fitness is committed to providing a supportive environment where members can work toward their personal fitness goals. Stay consistent, make use of the available facilities and resources, and enjoy your fitness journey!
